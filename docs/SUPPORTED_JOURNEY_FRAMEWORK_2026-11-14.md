## Implemented behavior

- The existing Job Management workspace shows a derived journey status and one next action. Work completion and money remain independent: only completed work with a settled final invoice and no unresolved visit/scope/payment blocker shows **Closed · Paid**. A deposit never completes work; internal costs never become customer pricing.
- A private tenant-linked ledger records new job, visit, revision-specific invoice opening and payment events. No historical event backfill. Shared crew rows deduplicate one visit; distinct partial/final payment transitions survive an atomic request. Existing work history and authorized workflow history share Job Records / Invoice Activity, with bounded paging. Jobs-only staff see no financial events, invoice IDs or balances.
- Invoice openings use the existing public capability/current revision/expiry/revocation checks. Only a current issued `sent` invoice advances to `viewed`. Scanner/preview openings are explicitly not human-read confirmation. Existing partial/paid states never regress from opening.
- Existing automatic and manual/Fin payment reminders hold for active/pending or review-required Stripe/Square operations. Settlement/void cancels eligible scheduled/queued reminders; in-flight provider outcomes remain available for reconciliation.
- Two owner-controlled email policies cover **future** booking confirmations and recorded-payment receipts. Both start off. Enabling requires Business Pro and an activated sender. Event-time customer/policy identity, frozen payloads, current role/plan/contact/appointment/payment checks, durable outbox IDs and bounded preparation preserve the existing quota/lease/provider-idempotency/signed-callback path. Failed/uncertain outcomes are not blindly resent; no historical sends occur on enable. Receipts describe verified cumulative applied funds and remaining balance.
- Rescheduling/cancellation reuses the saved appointment event. Authorized office users review the exact recipient and message, then choose **Send Finviant Email** once. Notification failure never rolls back a successful scheduling edit or repeats the edit. Existing Activity history displays the actual queued/accepted/delivered/review state. Provider acceptance and confirmed delivery are separate.
- Optional estimate payment terms are shown in the exact customer-visible canonical revision and carried to the final draft using its actual issue date. No global Net15/30 assumption. New linked final drafts without agreed terms require a due date before issuing. Existing pre-migration invoices retain their prior contract.
- Deposits block new booking only when explicitly marked required on their reviewed deposit invoice. Existing arranged visits remain; ordinary requested deposits do not silently acquire this rule. Issued requirements/terms require reviewed revisions rather than silent edits.
- Fin can propose explicitly requested estimate payment terms, show them for review and use the canonical approved document action once. Term days cannot supply an invented price/quantity. Existing OpenAI setup remains; background automation makes no AI calls.
- Ledger/policies/notices/terms metadata join the existing owner archive source. Existing archive authorization, historical snapshots and restore acceptance gates remain.
