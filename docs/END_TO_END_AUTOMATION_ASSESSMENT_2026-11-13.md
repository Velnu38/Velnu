# End-to-end automation assessment

Assessed November 13, 2026. Assessment and recommendations only. The owner's example is conceptual guidance, not authorization to implement its features or replace existing business rules.

## Verdict

Finviant has a real relational backend and several connected, transactional workflows. It does not yet meet the promise of a verified, hands-off customer-to-work-to-payment system. The remaining work combines missing lifecycle features, incomplete provider setup, clearer workflow presentation and real operational acceptance. Rebuilding the application or creating parallel copies of its existing records would add risk and repetition.

The currently verified beta includes the simplified invoice and estimate creators and revised Home. The beta branch was read again at `cc594261abb9aea23cf02872e8175b2085d55127`. Unified Job Management and the restored Fin buttons are a separate local development candidate, not a beta release. Neither release presence nor passing synthetic tests certifies customer messages, payment settlement or physical-phone operation.

## The actual reminder behavior

Fresh source defaults are **disabled**. Appointment reminders default to **48 elapsed hours before arrival**, with email or SMS selected by the owner. Invoice reminders default to **email at 2 days overdue, every 7 days, maximum 3**. Existing saved business settings and per-record overrides can differ. Source defaults do not establish this account's enabled state or successful delivery. The conceptual example does not authorize changing the established 48-hour appointment default.

Automatic invoice reminders require eligible Pro access, the owner's enabled rule, an active sender and current customer/invoice eligibility. Paid invoices are excluded when preparing and claiming work. That is substantial protection, but it is not evidence that all queued rows are canceled in the payment transaction or that every concurrency boundary has passed hosted acceptance. A provider-accepted or in-flight message cannot be recalled. The correct target is to suppress unclaimed future sends promptly, recheck before dispatch, preserve uncertainty and demonstrate payment-versus-dispatch races without duplicates.

## Recommended order

1. **Define one supported customer journey and its business policies.** Support estimate-first and direct-job/invoice entry using the same linked records. Specify deposit, agreed pricing, billable time/materials, remaining visits, payment terms and when final billing is appropriate. Keep job progress separate from invoice/payment state: completed work can still be unpaid, and a paid deposit does not mean the work is complete. An overall stage can be derived from both. Completion should end obsolete appointment messages while payment reminders continue only for an eligible remaining invoice balance.
2. **Finish and certify the existing integrations.** Resolve the recorded calendar queue failure, complete email readiness and merchant sandbox acceptance, and finish managed SMS only where selected. Give the owner an accurate Ready / Paused / Needs setup / Failed status with a direct remedy. General channel activation and controlled real transactions are separate authorized work, not part of this assessment.
3. **Connect lifecycle transitions through the existing transactional workflow and outboxes.** Cover approval, customer booking, worker assignment/change, reschedule/cancel, completion, invoice release, partial/full payment, refund and receipt. Give each automatic action a stable event identity, current-state check, retry policy, visible failure and reconciliation path. Preserve existing deduplication and document revisions. Do not build a second queue or assume a browser toast completes a durable workflow.
4. **Close the concrete feature gaps.** Add selected payment-reminder stages/templates; customer confirmation/change/cancellation/receipt coverage; invoice-open logging and owner alert; selected completion draft/autosend policy; and the agreed billable-price/progress model. Automatic sending should use the exact approved saved scope and authorized recipients. Unpriced, disputed, stale, uncertain or incomplete work should surface one clear resolution action.
5. **Make one job workspace the source of action.** Show customer/site, next visit/crew, completion blockers, approved charges, invoice balance and upcoming automation together, with one primary next action. Show routine messages in history and exceptions in attention. Add a separate board or automation feed only if it reduces actual navigation. Fin should open or prepare the same existing action, preserve entered data and explain exactly what happened or what is blocked.
6. **Prove the complete journey before advertising it as automatic.** Use isolated fixtures with four clients, five jobs and six invoices, plus a QA-only simulated-event toolbar if useful. Then exercise controlled real providers with two genuine business accounts and actual iPhone/Android devices. Include failure/recovery cases, not just a successful demonstration. Simulations must not write fake settlements or customer events into normal business data.
7. **Close operational and launch gates.** Retain the existing security/tenant-isolation work and prove hosted isolation, queue monitoring/alerts, recovery/reconciliation, backup/restore, capacity and per-workspace provider budgets. A green cron request or READY frontend deployment does not establish completed background work or production readiness. The registry still limits readiness to controlled private beta.

## What would count as end-to-end acceptance

Run one priced, approved, multi-visit job through customer booking, crew assignment, reschedule and notification, tasks/materials/change approval, completion, one correct final invoice with deposit credit, branded delivery, link-open event, partial payment, due/overdue reminder, final verified settlement, reminder suppression, receipt, financial closure and dashboard reconciliation. The owner should not re-enter customer, scope or agreed prices between these stages.

Repeat with duplicate clicks, concurrent edits, stale links, remaining visits, declined changes, changed recipients/opt-outs, channel failure, provider timeout, duplicate/out-of-order webhooks, lost payment return, refund and cross-business access attempts. Verify one intended record/action, correct balances, no unauthorized send or payment, visible uncertainty and recovery without guessing or blind resend. Record action counts/time and phone observations; evaluate Fin against the same journey.
