# Interactive dashboard

The calendar stays at the top of the dashboard. Week and Month controls replace the redundant Today and Open schedule actions. Both views use business-local dates, navigate periods, display work counts, and select a day's jobs, scheduled documents, and due reminders. Month view includes a month picker. Work spanning midnight appears on every overlapping day; an exact end at midnight does not spill into the next day. The selected range loads once, so changing days inside it does not require another request. Paging verifies complete results; incomplete reads display an error.

Financial performance supports 1, 3, 6 (default), and 12 calendar months ending in the current month. The one-month view plots individual days; longer views plot months. Selecting a chart point narrows the summary. Income, Expenses, and Net total expand a searchable list with complete pagination, dates, descriptions, categories, payment methods, notes, source labels, and links to accessible source records. Net total shows income less expenses and both sides of the calculation. All amounts are recorded USD cash activity; invoices are not income until payment is recorded. QuickBooks is kept separate to avoid double-counting.

Ranged queries keep tenant filters, existing RLS, and role/module permissions. Payment timestamps are converted to the business timezone; date-only manual entries retain their entered calendar dates. Totals use integer cents. Later-page failures disable totals instead of presenting partial or invented zero values. Source links respect access to the related module. Job links resolve the specific job, including jobs outside the initial list page.

Visual changes use light blue/teal cards, an interactive line chart, clear hover/focus feedback, keyboard-operable chart points and calendar controls, and reduced-motion support. Dashboard Refresh reloads the summary, calendar, and financial data together. The prior owner-only Settings, payment options, and team permissions remain in place. The site remains private.
